Help Center & FAQs
How do I join a tournament?
Go to the Arena tab, browse upcoming matches, tap Join Match, provide your Free Fire In-Game Name (IGN) and Character UID, and confirm your entry.
Where do I get Room ID and Password?
When the match status changes to 🔴 LIVE NOW (usually 10-15 minutes before the schedule), the Room ID & Password will automatically appear on the tournament detail page and inside the Match Room tab.
How does the Referral Reward work?
Share your unique Referral Code from the Invite & Earn page. When friends sign up using your code, every 5 invites unlocks a 1 Free Tournament Entry Pass 🎟️ + bonus cash rewards!
How to deposit and withdraw money?
Deposits are instant via UddoktaPay (bKash, Nagad, Rocket). Withdrawals can be requested from the Wallet tab and are processed to your personal mobile number within 1-24 hours.
What if a match is cancelled?
If an official match is cancelled, entry fees are 100% refunded automatically to all joined participants' wallets.
